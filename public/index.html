Outcome 2

📚 Learn & Do: Sustainable Biomass Knowledge Base

Educational resources, scientific conversion standards, and field safety protocols.

⚖️ Important Scientific Distinction

Educational & On-Farm Guidance: Methods like composting, vermicomposting, and flame-cap biochar are farmer-executable on-site.
Industrial Bio-Refinery Processing: Advanced pathways like 2G Bio-Ethanol (enzymatic hydrolysis + fermentation) and Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) require specialized industrial processing infrastructure. AgriAI provides decision support and logistical matchmaking without claiming direct physical conversion in software.

🧪 How 2G Bio-Ethanol is Made

Step 1: Pre-treatment: High-pressure steam or dilute acid breaks the rigid lignin-hemicellulose sheath.
Step 2: Enzymatic Hydrolysis: Cellulase enzymes break complex cellulose chains into simple glucose and xylose sugars.
Step 3: Fermentation: Genetically optimized yeast strains (e.g. Saccharomyces cerevisiae) ferment sugars into ethanol.
Step 4: Distillation: Distilled to 99.5% fuel-grade ethanol blended with petrol under India's Ethanol Blended Petrol (E20) program.

✋ Quick Field Moisture Test (Hand-Squeeze)

Dry (<15%): Straw crackles when bent and breaks cleanly. No moisture on palm when squeezed tightly. Ideal for fuel plants and briquetting.
Semi-Dry (15–25%): Straw bends without breaking easily. Slight cool feeling on palm. Suitable for CBG digesters.
Wet (>30%): Water droplets squeeze out between fingers. Unusable for burning; immediately route to composting or vermicomposting.
